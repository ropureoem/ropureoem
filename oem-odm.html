OEM / ODM — How We Work

We supply finished water purification systems under your brand. You bring the market and the brand; we supply the model, the branding and the packing.

  1. 1. Choose a model. Browse the catalogue and pick the form factor and model that fits your line.
  2. 2. Tell us your market. Target market, model of interest and your brand requirements — through the Get a Quote form, by email or on WhatsApp.
  3. 3. We confirm the details. Specifications, branding, packaging, instructions and the certificate list for that model are confirmed per order, before you commit.
  4. 4. Approve a sample. Confirm the sample, including branding and packing, before bulk production.
  5. 5. Bulk order under your brand. Production, quality checks and shipment are arranged around the approved sample.

What you can customise

Branding and logo, packaging and market-specific instructions, finish and configuration options where the model allows, and replacement cartridges. Every change is confirmed against the approved sample — nothing is promised until it is confirmed for that model.

Project FAQ

How do I plan water purifier parts for my OEM model?

Share the model, target market and replacement schedule with your OEM water filter manufacturer. Water purification parts and water filter components require model-specific compatibility checks. Availability and specifications: Confirmed per order.

Can I request an ultra high purity purifier?

Send your feed-water analysis, intended use and required output purity for review. The catalogue does not establish suitability for ultra high purity applications. Feasibility and specifications: Confirmed per order.

Can I specify industrial reverse osmosis hot water requirements?

Provide the intended process, flow rate and temperature requirements. Household catalogue models do not establish suitability for industrial reverse osmosis hot water applications. Feasibility and specifications: Confirmed per order.
