Voltage and plug by country

Voltage and frequency differ by country. They are part of the order, not an afterthought.

Tell us the target country

Voltage and frequency are configurable to the target country. Tell us the country and the plug your market uses when you enquire.

Confirmed per model and order

Voltage / frequency and other configurations are confirmed per model and order — the same wording appears on every product page. Nothing is assumed from a previous order.

Certification follows the market

The certificate list is also per market — see Certification. Tell us the model and target market and we will confirm the exact list before you order.
