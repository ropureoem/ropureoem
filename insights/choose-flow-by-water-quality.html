How to choose flow rate by water quality

Flow rate is the first number to settle. It decides whether a model can keep up with your customers' daily use.

Start with the feed water

Ask for the feed-water quality of the target market — hardness and total dissolved solids are the usual starting points. If you need ultra-high-purity water, send your feed-water analysis first — the same applies on the OEM / ODM page.

Match capacity to demand

A countertop model such as RP-C09 is rated 50 / 75 / 100 GPD. A countertop dispenser such as RP-C04 produces 30 L/h. A high-capacity under-sink model such as RP-U03 is rated 1000 / 1200 / 1600 GPD. Choose the capacity band that fits the household or office — not the largest number on the page.

Confirm before you order

Flow rates and capacities are confirmed per model and order. Tell us the target market and expected daily use in the Get a Quote form.
