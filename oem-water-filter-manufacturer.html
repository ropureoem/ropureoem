OEM Water Filter Manufacturer — Water Filters Built for Your Brand

As an OEM water filter manufacturer, ROPureOEM supplies finished water filters and purification systems under your brand — under-sink RO, countertop RO, faucet filters, shower filters and water softeners. You bring the market and the brand; we supply the model, the branding and the packing.

Water filters we manufacture

Every model below is a confirmed catalogue model, supplied as OEM / ODM for brand owners and distributors. Specifications are confirmed per model and order.

  • Under-sink RO systems — 15 models for the kitchen, from 400 GPD to 1400 GPD, with and without heating.
  • Countertop RO systems — countertop purifiers, including heating models and bean-to-cup coffee combinations.
  • Faucet filters — faucet-mounted filters for municipal tap water.
  • Shower filters — portable handheld, inline and rectangular shower filters.
  • Water softeners — whole-house softening for homes and light commercial use.

Two ways to work with an OEM water filter manufacturer

OEM — start from a ready model. Pick a confirmed model and make it yours: logo, colour, filters, faucet, packaging.

ODM — build to your brief. Customise structure, function and mould to your brief. Every brief needs an engineering assessment first.

How a project runs

  1. 1. Choose a model. Browse the catalogue and pick the form factor and model that fits your line.
  2. 2. Tell us your market. Target market, model of interest and your brand requirements — through the Get a Quote form, by email or on WhatsApp.
  3. 3. We confirm the details. Specifications, branding, packaging, instructions and the certificate list for that model are confirmed per order, before you commit.
  4. 4. Approve a sample. Confirm the sample, including branding and packing, before bulk production.
  5. 5. Bulk order under your brand. Production, quality checks and shipment are arranged around the approved sample.

Water filter manufacturer FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for OEM water filters?

The first order is 200 units per model, and reorders are 500 units.

Can I order a sample before a bulk order?

Yes — samples are paid, and the sample fee is refunded on your first bulk order. Approve the sample, including branding and packing, before bulk production.

How long is the lead time?

Lead time is confirmed per quotation. As a guide, shipment is 40 days after sample approval.

Can you put my brand on the product and the packaging?

Yes — logo, colour, filters, faucet and packaging are customised to your brand, and confirmed against the approved sample.

Which certifications can I get for my market?

Certification is confirmed per model and market. Tell us your model and target market and we confirm the exact list before you order.
