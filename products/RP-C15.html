RP-C15

Sparkling-water & hydrogen-water countertop family (P90 / P90-1) — two variants, one page.

RP-C15 is a countertop RO purifier family with two variants. P90 is the sparkling-water model — sparkling, iced and hot water from one unit. P90-1 is the hydrogen-water model — hydrogen-rich water with iced and hot water. Both are compressor-cooled, use RO filtration with UV sterilisation, and work with a built-in tank or a plumbed connection. Specifications are confirmed per model and order.

Specifications below are listed per variant — P90 sparkling-water model first, then P90-1 hydrogen-water model. All figures are taken from the manufacturer specification sheets.
