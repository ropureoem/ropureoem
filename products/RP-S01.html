RP-S01 / RP-S02 / RP-S06

Handheld shower filter (H503) — three variants. RP-S01 leads the family.

RP-S01, RP-S02 and RP-S06 are three variants of the same handheld shower filter and share the specification values below. They differ only in colour and body: RP-S01 (white + rose-gold, 73 × 253 × 79 mm); RP-S02 (grey + rose-gold); RP-S06 (slimmer body with a chrome ring, 86 × 261 × 81 mm). Specifications are confirmed per model and order.
