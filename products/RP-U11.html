Under-Sink RO Systems

RP-U11

Three-cartridge RO purifier (AM606R)

RP-U11 is a three-cartridge under-sink RO family led by the RP-U13 (600/800/1000G, 427 mm tall, 6000 L rated volume), alongside the RP-U11 (fixed 1000G, 400 mm tall) — plus two higher-capacity RP-U13 variants (1000/1200G and 800/1000G). Specifications are confirmed per model and order.

Get a Quote for RP-U11

Voltage / frequency is configurable to the target country. Other configurations are confirmed per order.

Four options — quote as RP-U11 and specify the option (RP-U11 1000G, RP-U13 600/800/1000G, RP-U13 1000/1200G or RP-U13 800/1000G). Rated power for the RP-U13 main option (65 / 95 / 105 W) is pending factory confirmation.
