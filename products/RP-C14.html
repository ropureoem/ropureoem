Countertop Systems

RP-C14 / RP-C05

Sparkling-water series (W61) — eight variants. RP-C14 (W61) leads the series.

RP-C14, RP-C05 and the W62 finish are three of the eight variants of the sparkling-water series. The series also includes the W64 finish — the same model as RP-C14 (W61), panel finish only — and four further bubble variants (W65, W68, W69 and D1), whose specifications are confirmed per order. The three pictured variants share the specification values below and differ only in display and front panel: RP-C14 (W61) has a small LED touchscreen and a dark-grey striped panel; RP-C05 (W60) has a 7-inch full-colour touchscreen and a dark brushed-metal panel; RP-C14 (W62) is the same model as RP-C14 (W61) with a black-gold vertical-stripe panel. Specifications are confirmed per model and order.

Confirmed specifications below apply to RP-C14, RP-C05, the W62 variant and the W64 finish.
