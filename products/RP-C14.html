Sparkling-water series — one appearance, three variants. RP-C14 (W61) leads the series.

RP-C14, RP-C05 and RP-C06 share one appearance and differ in sparkling-water configuration. RP-C14 and RP-C06 specifications are confirmed per order. Specifications are confirmed per model and order.
