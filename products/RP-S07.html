RP-S07 / RP-S08 / RP-S05 / RP-S09

Multi-mode shower filter (JK-C1) — four models

RP-S07, RP-S08, RP-S05 and RP-S09 are four models in the same multi-mode shower filter family. RP-S07 (white-purple), RP-S08 (beige) and RP-S05 (black) are the same model in three colours; RP-S09 (beige, gold ring) uses the same mould with a composite PP cotton + vitamin C cartridge. Specifications are confirmed per model and order.
