Countertop Systems

RP-C24

Built-in cold & hot water dispenser (AM2564-H) — three options. RP-C24 leads the series.

RP-C24 is a built-in (embedded) cold & hot water dispenser family: a 280–325 mm deep unit that installs flush into kitchen cabinetry, with compressor cooling, 3300W thick-film instant heating and flow-through UV sterilisation. Three options share the same platform and differ in display, filtration and ice-making — the cold & hot dispenser (large colour touch screen, 6 temperature presets + 1 custom setting, 595 × 280 × 450 mm); the TFT-screen purifier (radar-sensing auto-wake, 1000G, 5000 L rated volume, 595 × 325 × 455 mm); and the bullet-ice purifier (5 kg/24h bullet ice, 1000G, 4000 L rated volume, 595 × 300 × 450 mm). Specifications are confirmed per model and order.
