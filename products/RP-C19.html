Slim countertop RO purifier with glass jug (AM2615R)

RP-C19 — Slim countertop RO purifier with glass jug. Key specification: RO capacity 75 / 100 GPD. Specifications are confirmed per model and order.

RP-C19 is a slim countertop RO purifier with a glass pure-water jug. The jug has a built-in mineralisation cartridge, and the 3 L raw-water tank sits on the right side for easy removal.
