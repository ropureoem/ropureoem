RP-C18

Countertop RO with compressor cooling and ice maker (P80)

RP-C18 — Countertop RO with compressor cooling and ice maker. Key specification: RO capacity 150 GPD. Specifications are confirmed per model and order.

RP-C18 is a countertop RO purifier with compressor cooling and an ice maker. It dispenses 10°C chilled water, makes ice cubes (2 per cycle, 3–5 stored) and offers seven hot-water settings with an AI sensor.
