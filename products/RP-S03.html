About RP-S03

RP-S03 is a shower filter that treats the water before it reaches the shower head. It comes in two head shapes — a rectangular head and a ring head — that share the same core specifications. Cartridge options include ACF, vitamin C, scale-inhibition media and KDF, so the filter can be matched to the water a brand's customers have. It is made for brand owners who want a simple shower product to add to a bathroom line.

Why buyers choose it

  • Cartridge choice: ACF, vitamin C + ACF, scale-inhibition media + KDF + ACF, or a PP + vitamin C aroma cartridge on the ring-head variant.
  • Shower-ready flow of 5–7 L/min at a working pressure of 0.1–0.4 MPa, for municipal tap water at 5–60°C.
  • Cartridge life of approximately 3 months, giving a clear replacement cycle.
  • Two head shapes from one family: rectangular (ABS, painted finish) or ring (PC + ABS with a silicone sealing ring).

Best for

  • Households that want filtered water in the shower.
  • Hotels and rental apartments fitting out bathrooms.
  • Brands adding a shower filter to a bathroom or water-treatment line.
