RP-F10 / RP-F11

Faucet-mounted water filter (JK-B1) — integrated and split

RP-F10 (integrated) and RP-F11 (split) are two models in the same faucet-mounted water filter family, sharing the same filter cartridge. Key specification: Format Integrated or split faucet filter. Specifications are confirmed per model and order.
