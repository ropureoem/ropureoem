About RP-C12

RP-C12 is a countertop water purifier (T01) with RO filtration (PCB + RO) at 75 GPD and a rated total purified water volume of 2000 L. It has a 4 L raw-water tank and a 1 L pure-water tank, heats with 2100 W heating power, and dispenses at 100/85/60/45 °C plus ambient, in 150/300/450 mL volumes. It measures 314 × 165 × 396 mm.

Why buyers choose it

  • 75 GPD RO with PCB + RO filtration; rated total purified water volume 2000 L.
  • Five water temperatures: 100/85/60/45 °C plus ambient, with 150/300/450 mL volume settings.
  • Purified-water flow of 1.2 L/min (ambient) / 0.4 L/min (hot); pure-water flow 0.2 L/min.
  • 4 L raw-water tank and 1 L pure-water tank; Class I protection against electric shock; feed-water temperature 5–45 °C.

Best for

  • Homes that want multi-temperature hot water from a countertop unit.
  • Small offices that need hot water at set temperatures and volumes.
  • Brands that want a compact countertop RO at 314 × 165 × 396 mm.
