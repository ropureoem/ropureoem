About RP-C08

RP-C08 is an all-in-one cartridge countertop purifier (M2110RN) built around a single 5-in-1 composite RO cartridge. It heats water instantly, with the heating configuration set per order. RO capacity and dimensions are confirmed per order, so buyers quote the model and confirm the specification with their order.

Why buyers choose it

  • One cartridge does the whole job — 5-in-1 composite RO filtration in a single cartridge.
  • Instant heating; configuration by order.
  • Countertop form — a single self-contained unit.
  • RO capacity and dimensions confirmed per order.

Best for

  • Buyers who want a simple countertop RO with a single cartridge to replace.
  • Homes that want instant hot water from a countertop unit.
  • Brands that want a straightforward countertop model and will confirm capacity per order.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

The first order is 200 units per model, and reorders are 500 units.

Can I order a sample before a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are ready in 15 days, and the sample fee is refunded on your first bulk order. Bulk orders take 40 days after sample approval and ship FOB Shenzhen.

Which specifications are confirmed before ordering?

Filtration is the 5-in-1 composite RO cartridge and heating is instant. RO capacity and dimensions are confirmed per order — quote as RP-C08 and confirm them with your order.
