RP-C16

Instant-heating countertop purifier (RF / RH)

RP-C16 — Instant-heating countertop purifier. Key specification: RO capacity 75 GPD. Specifications are confirmed per model and order.

RP-C16 covers the RF and RH instant-heating countertop models. Both use a 75 GPD instant-heating system with no cooling and no ice maker. The RF model offers three temperature settings (45 / 85 / 95°C), 150 / 300 mL dispensing and a child lock; the RH model pairs the same instant-heating system with an external jug that can be chilled in a refrigerator.
