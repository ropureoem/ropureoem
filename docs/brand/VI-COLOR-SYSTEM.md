# ROPureOEM visual identity

The uploaded ROPureOEM_Final_Petrol_Cyan.zip is the approved source. Preserve the supplied SVG and PNG bytes. Do not redraw, recolor, change proportions, or replace the circular ripple/wave symbol or coral dot. No forest-green/amber identity.

## Core palette

| Name | HEX | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Teal / Petrol | #00343D | Primary brand surface, headers, footer, hero, body text on white. |
| Ice Cyan | #8CE2E4 | RO and ripple in dark logo; highlights, links on petrol, filled buttons with petrol text. |
| Coral | #FF786C | Approved dot and small emphasis accents. Use sparingly; do not make it the main surface. |
| White | #FFFFFF | PureOEM on petrol; primary light surface and dark-surface body text. |

Suggested visual balance: petrol and white dominate, cyan provides emphasis, coral stays a small accent. These are layout guidelines, not permission to recolor logo artwork.

## Approved background variants

Dark artwork uses #00343D background, #8CE2E4 ripple/RO, #FFFFFF PureOEM, #F5F5EF wave, and #FF786C dot.

Light artwork is supplied with #FFFFFF background, #168C96 ripple/RO, #153B43 wave, #15313E PureOEM, and the same #FF786C dot. These exact supporting colors come from the approved ZIP; retain them. Do not apply ice cyan or white lettering to white surfaces. Use the supplied light variant.

## Logo handling

Files are in public/brand/. Logo canvases: 1600 × 480. Icon canvases: 1024 × 1024. SVG + PNG, light + dark: eight original files. Use the full logo in headers/footers and the icon alone for avatars/favicons. Keep the aspect ratio and built-in whitespace; never stretch or crop. Leave additional breathing room near other content. SVG wordmarks retain the source font stack (Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif); use the supplied PNG when exact raster typography must be preserved across platforms. The originals are intentionally not outlined or regenerated.

## Interface usage

Use styles/brand-tokens.css as the shared source for CSS roles and docs/brand/colors.json for machine-readable values. White/petrol for reading; cyan/petrol for primary actions; coral/petrol only for small highlights. Use petrol focus rings on white and cyan focus rings on petrol, with an offset. Pair status colors with words or icons; coral alone must not communicate an error. Do not use cyan text on white or white text on coral for ordinary text.

Contact address: info@ropureoem.com, including visible text and mailto links.
