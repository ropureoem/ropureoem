About ROPureOEM

ROPureOEM is a water purifier OEM / ODM supplier working with brand owners and distributors worldwide.

Our range covers countertop RO purifiers, under-sink RO systems, water softeners, shower filters and faucet filters. Each model is supplied as a finished unit under your brand, with branding, packaging and market-specific instructions confirmed per order.

We do not sell retail to end users. If you are a brand owner or distributor adding a water line, or replacing an existing line with a ready model, send us a quote request and tell us your target market and the model you are interested in.
